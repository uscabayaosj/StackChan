# Saoirse companion

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users

Saoirse’s owner uses an iPhone to manage the robot at home and away.

## Product Purpose

Keep Wi-Fi networks, AI configurations and conversations available to revisit. Provide remote controls for Saoirse and direct viewing and capture through her camera.

## Operating Context

Wi-Fi setup happens within Bluetooth range. Remote controls, profiles, transcripts and the camera use the Saoirse backend. Access away from home is required.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Saoirse. Her on-device face uses two softly rounded phosphor-green eyes on black.
